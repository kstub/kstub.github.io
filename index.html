Kevin Stubbs’ Homepage

Adventures in Mathematics and Physics

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Mathematics Department at UC Berkeley working with Lin Lin. Before that I was a Simons Postdoctoral Fellow at the Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics working with Stan Osher.

I received my PhD from Duke University in 2021 advised by Jianfeng Lu.

My main research focus lies at the intersection between applied math and the quantum sciences with a particular focus on efficient algorithms and high dimensional problems. I am particularly interested in connections between algorithms and condensed matter physics.
